How it works

Sign up. Send a message.
Everything else is automatic.

Gmail account and phone number. That's all. No credit card. No onboarding tutorial.

→
02
Text or message

Send anything to your KLAIVE number — a note, a date, an amount, a health reading.

→
03
Your calendar

Dates and appointments land on your calendar automatically. No manual entry. Ever.

Auto indexing

Features

One input.
Three dashboards.

Calendar sync

Any date or time in your message becomes a calendar event. Syncs with Google Calendar automatically.

Auto classification

Finance, Health, Tasks. KLAIVE reads the context and routes information to the right dashboard without prompts.

Live overview

Your dashboard updates the moment a message arrives. No refresh. No manual input. Logged and visible instantly.

Philosophy
"Raw input flows in.
Structured value flows out.
The system stays quiet."

KLAIVE is built on three principles — transformation, balance, and catalysis. Information enters as clay and leaves as structure. The system never interrupts. It simply works.
