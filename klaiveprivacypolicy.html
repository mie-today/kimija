Privacy Policy

KLAIVE · Effective date: June 11, 2026 · Last updated: June 11, 2026

Our core promise: Your data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. KLAIVE has no ads. Your information exists for one purpose only — to organize it for you.

1. Who we are

KLAIVE ("we," "us," or "our") is an information organization service operated from San Francisco, CA, United States. KLAIVE allows you to send information by text message (SMS) or other messaging channels, which is then automatically organized into your calendar, documents, and personal dashboards (Finance, Health, Tasks).

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@klaive.io.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your full name, Gmail email address, and mobile phone number. Your phone number is used to identify messages you send to KLAIVE.

Message content. The text messages, notes, and files you send to your KLAIVE number or through the KLAIVE interface. This may include schedule information, financial records, health-related records, tasks, and memos that you choose to send.

Google account data. With your explicit consent via Google OAuth, we access your Google Calendar to create and sync events, and your Gmail address for account identification. We request only the minimum scopes required for these functions. We do not read your email inbox.

Usage data. Basic technical information such as log-in timestamps, device type, and feature usage, used solely to operate and improve the service.

3. How we use your information

  • To parse, classify, and organize the messages you send into your calendar, documents, and dashboards
  • To create and sync calendar events with your Google Calendar
  • To deliver daily schedule summaries to you by email or text message
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the service
  • To communicate with you about your account and service updates

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data — to anyone, for any reason
  • We never share your data with third parties for their own purposes
  • We never use your data — including any health-related records — for advertising or marketing profiles
  • We never display ads in any KLAIVE product
  • We never use your content to train models for any party outside of providing the KLAIVE service to you

5. Health-related information

Some users choose to send health-related records (such as weight, blood pressure, or glucose readings) to KLAIVE. We treat all such records as sensitive information. They are encrypted at rest, are never shared with any third party, are never used for advertising, and are accessible only to you. KLAIVE is a personal record-keeping tool and is not a medical device; it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

6. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud Platform infrastructure in the United States. All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS. Internal access to user data is restricted to the minimum necessary for operating the service, controlled through identity and access management policies, and logged.

Please note that standard SMS is not an end-to-end encrypted channel. Messages are encrypted once they reach our systems, but the transmission of an SMS over carrier networks is subject to the security of those networks. If you prefer, you may submit information through the KLAIVE web interface instead.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual records at any time within the service. If you delete your account, all associated personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct, and the right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA. To exercise any right, contact privacy@klaive.io. We respond within the timelines required by applicable law.

9. Google API Services disclosure

KLAIVE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide user-facing features of KLAIVE, is never transferred to third parties, is never used for advertising, and is never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

10. Children's privacy

KLAIVE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by email or in-service notice at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
